# Contributing

Thank you for helping make the FlatSat engineering package reproducible.

## Before submitting

1. Open an issue for a proposed new deliverable, change to scope, or unresolved technical question.
2. Work on a branch and submit a pull request. Include the purpose, affected unit/configuration, evidence, and review needed.
3. Mark content as **planned**, **in work**, **tested**, or **accepted**. Do not describe an intended result as completed.
4. Identify the author, source, revision/date, and license for each contributed artifact.
5. Do not submit confidential, personally identifying, export-controlled, NDA-covered, or third-party content without documented permission for public release.
6. Do not put credentials, access tokens, private keys, serial numbers tied to a person, or private contact details in the repository or its issue tracker.

## Licensing and authority

By submitting original documentation or other non-software content, a contributor confirms that they have authority to make it available under CC BY 4.0. By submitting original software, a contributor confirms that they have authority to make it available under the MIT License. Contributors must disclose any third-party material and its terms. A pull request is not accepted until maintainers have resolved authorship, ownership, and release permission questions.

This repository does not impose a separate copyright assignment or claim ownership of a contributor's work. Institutional or course requirements may apply independently; contributors should resolve those requirements with their institution before submission.

## Review

Maintainers review technical accuracy, reproducibility, provenance, license status, and project-scope fit. Hardware changes and verification claims require review by the responsible engineering authority. Merging a pull request does not by itself signify formal verification, safety approval, or flight readiness.
